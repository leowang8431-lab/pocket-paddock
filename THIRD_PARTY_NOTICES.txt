ENGINE MECHANICAL CYCLE DATA (0.11)
Source: "F1 Engine Ignition" by lmnmrn, Freesound #197219.
https://freesound.org/people/lmnmrn/sounds/197219/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication).
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source description says recorded with RODE NTG-2; exact vehicle, engine and
model year are unverified and are not claimed by Pocket Paddock.
Changes: decoded to mono PCM; phase-synchronous trimmed averaging of 42/103
mechanical cycles at source segments 3.5-4.5 and 6.0-7.3 seconds; harmonic
projection and fundamental phase alignment. Only derived coefficients are
bundled in src/engine-cycles.ts. Runtime has no source-audio downloads.
The official Formula 1 Norris/Monza video was used for reference analysis
only. No samples from that official video are distributed with the game.

PIT-STOP PREVIEW RECORDINGS (V5)
All three source recordings are CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

wheel-gun.wav: Air Impact Wrench by sevenbsb, Freesound #349398.
https://freesound.org/people/sevenbsb/sounds/349398/
Source description: FORTGPRO-FG3200 air impact wrench, SONY ICD-PX312.
Changes (V5): several recorded impact sections joined with 16ms crossfades
into a continuous 1-second sample at original pitch; mono PCM16 32000Hz;
high-pass 95Hz, low-pass 2700Hz, head/tail fades and gain adjustment.

jack.wav: metal clanks.wav by rsellick, Freesound #545547.
https://freesound.org/people/rsellick/sounds/545547/
Real metallic clanks used as jack-contact and latch Foley.
Changes: trim 0.666-0.986 seconds; mono PCM16 32000Hz; high-pass 90Hz,
low-pass 3100Hz; 1ms/45ms fades; gain 1.1.

tyre.wav: Rubber Band Thud.wav by datsinoil, Freesound #689246.
https://freesound.org/people/datsinoil/sounds/689246/
A real gymnastic rubber band recording is used for tyre-contact Foley.
Changes: short mono 32000Hz PCM16 trim, filtering, gain and fades.
Source processing details and sample metrics are included in the V5 delivery.

None of these effects is claimed to be an actual Formula 1 team's pit-stop
recording. No broadcast audio, wind/noise bed or electronic beep is included.
